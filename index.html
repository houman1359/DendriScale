Size accounting, evaluation conditions & frontier definition

The default axis shows whole-model bytes retained: 100% = original size; 25% = 4× smaller. Byte measurements include precision and executable connectivity. They are not active-parameter percentages or runtime measurements.

A frontier point has no measured alternative in this panel that is both smaller and at least as good, with one strict improvement. This does not establish statistical superiority or override a failed gate. Local fidelity is not whole-model capability.
